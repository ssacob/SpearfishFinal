Safety

Important safety guidelines for spearfishing

Spearfishing Safety Guide

  • Always dive with a buddy for increased safety and support.
  • Check your equipment before entering the water to ensure everything is functioning properly.
  • Be aware of your surroundings, including currents, waves, and marine life.
  • Practice proper breath-hold techniques and never push your limits.
  • Respect local regulations and marine protected areas to preserve the environment.
